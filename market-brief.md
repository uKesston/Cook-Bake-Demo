# Cook & Bake Academy — Market Brief

## Positioning

Hands-on cooking and baking courses in small cohorts across two Singapore campuses, from accessible one-session workshops to specialist multi-week programmes. This positioning reflects the proposed catalogue; market demand and willingness to pay remain unvalidated.

## Customer personas

- **Ambitious home cook:** seeks practical skills and progression in bread, pastry, knife work, or cuisines.
- **Experience-seeking pair or visitor:** wants a social, hands-on cooking experience, often with a local or regional food angle.
- **Cautious first-timer or gift buyer:** prefers a beginner-friendly, shorter workshop with a clear duration and take-home result.

These are hypotheses, not measured segments; audience sizes and willingness to pay are UNKNOWN.

## Five courses to launch first

1. Knife Skills & Kitchen Essentials — **S$160**
2. Cookie & Biscuit Baking — **S$180**
3. Macaron Masterclass — **S$420**
4. Thai Street Food Cooking — **S$540**
5. Artisan Sourdough Bread Baking — **S$680**

The mix tests accessible entry classes, specialist baking, and a cuisine-based experience; it is not a demand ranking.

## Pricing notes

Proposed catalogue fees span **S$160–S$1,580**. Public market examples range from community workshops to longer institute programmes, but formats, durations, subsidies, and inclusions differ. The catalogue gives course weeks, not meeting counts or contact hours; per-session comparisons are UNKNOWN. Show total meetings, contact hours, materials and ingredient inclusions, take-home outcomes, and instructor credentials beside each fee.

## Website conversion priorities

1. Make duration, session count, campus, inclusions, and take-home outcome clear beside every price.
2. Make the low-commitment beginner workshops easy to find and book as first classes.
3. Track page-to-booking conversion and seat fill by course and intake; do not present broad tourism or festival figures as course-demand proof.
